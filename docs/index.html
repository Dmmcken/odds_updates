How the estimates work

The lottery publishes how many prizes of each size are left, and each game’s rules PDF says how many tickets were printed and how many prizes there were at the start. The most common prizes (such as the smallest ones) show what share of a game has already been sold. From that I estimate how many tickets are left, then divide the remaining prize money by the cost of those tickets to get the average payout per $1. Click any game to see its remaining prizes and the estimated odds of each.

Games marked rough have no usable rules PDF, so their numbers come from the odds printed on the lottery’s page and are less reliable. Prizes only count as gone once they are claimed, so the true number of tickets left is a little lower than shown.
